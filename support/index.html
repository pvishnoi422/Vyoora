Help with Vyoora

Support

For account, privacy or app questions, email pripriv.23@gmail.com.

What to include

Describe what happened, the screen you were using, your app version and your iOS version. Include an error code if one is displayed. Do not send your password, verification codes, API keys or private photos.

Signing in

Fashion features require an account with a verified email address. After creating an account, check your email for the verification message, including your spam folder. Account-specific help may require verification that you own the account.

A preview failed or was withheld

Generation can fail, be cancelled, or be withheld when the automated garment and safety review does not pass. A failed attempt does not guarantee that no provider processing occurred. Private testing also has shared spending controls and account limits; a limit message may require waiting or contacting support.

Deleting photos or your account

Delete wardrobe items from Wardrobe. Delete a reference photo to revoke its access and remove associated previews and saved looks. Delete a saved look from My Looks to remove that saved copy.

To delete your fashion account, open Wardrobe, choose Delete account, re-enter your password and confirm. If deletion reports a cleanup problem, retry the same deletion; processing remains blocked while cleanup is pending. To remove local skincare results, clear History separately. If you cannot access your account, email support for help.

Expiry and saved looks

Reference photos and unsaved previews have seven-day access lifetimes. A separately saved look is available for 30 days from saving. Access expiry can happen before physical cloud cleanup. Deleting a reference photo explicitly also removes its associated saved looks.

Save to Photos and Share

These exports require the native iOS app. The web preview does not offer native Photos saving or the iOS share sheet. Exports include the Vyoora AI-preview watermark, preserve image dimensions and leave the private stored original unchanged. Copies you export or share must be managed in Photos or the chosen destination.
