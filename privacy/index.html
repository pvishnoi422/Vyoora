Privacy policy

This policy describes Vyoora 2’s skincare and private fashion features. Processing depends on the features you choose and the consent you give.

Who operates Vyoora

Vyoora is published by Priya Vishnoi. For privacy questions, access requests or help deleting data, contact pripriv.23@gmail.com.

Sign-in and private fashion content

Google Firebase handles fashion-account sign-in using your email address and password. Vyoora’s backend uses your verified Firebase identity to authorize access to your own wardrobe images, garment details, reference photos, generated previews, saved looks and associated records. Google Cloud hosts the backend, private image storage and job and usage records. Photo reads require authenticated requests; this flow does not create public photo links.

Passwords are submitted to Firebase for sign-in or recent reauthentication. The app keeps session tokens in memory rather than scan history or device preferences.

AI outfit previews

After explicit consent, Vyoora sends the adult reference photo and garment you select to Google’s Gemini models on Google Cloud in the United States. A separate request to the same provider compares the generated image with the selected photos for garment fidelity, coverage and image safety. Failed or uncertain checks withhold the preview. Automated checks can miss defects. These images are AI previews and do not predict exact physical fit or sizing.

Only use photos you have permission to process, and follow the app’s adult-photo confirmation. Vyoora does not send fashion photos to OpenAI, Vercel or a social network through this flow.

Skincare scans and chat

After your consent, skincare features send ingredient-label photos and product type, or chat messages and recent conversation history, through Vyoora’s backend to OpenAI. Label pixels are processed in backend memory. The last five completed scan results are stored on your device and can be cleared from History. Chat history is held in the current screen’s memory. These local skincare records are separate from the fashion account.

Do not include unnecessary personal or sensitive information in label photos or chat. Skincare output is informational and does not replace professional medical advice.

How long content remains available

  • Wardrobe photos remain until you delete them.
  • Reference photos and unsaved previews become inaccessible after seven days.
  • Saving a look with separate consent creates a private copy available for 30 days from saving. Natural reference-photo expiry does not shorten that saved copy’s own lifetime.
  • Generation, consent and security or spending records may remain for up to 65 days. Usage controls include hashed identifiers and reservation counters; job and reference records also contain identifiers and status. They do not contain image pixels.

Access deadlines and physical cloud deletion are different. Lifecycle cleanup can occur after content becomes inaccessible rather than at the exact deadline.

Your deletion controls

You can delete wardrobe items, reference photos and saved looks in the app. Explicit reference-photo deletion revokes access first, cancels associated work and removes its previews and saved looks. Deleting a saved look removes that saved copy; delete the reference photo to remove associated generation content as well. If cleanup fails, revoked content remains blocked and deletion can be retried.

Delete account is available from Wardrobe and requires recent password reauthentication. It blocks new processing first, removes private fashion media and generation/reference records, then deletes the Firebase identity. If cleanup fails, processing stays blocked and deletion can be retried. Deletion does not reset shared security or spending counters. Clear local skincare History separately.

Provider retention and exported copies

OpenAI and Google may retain inputs and outputs in independent abuse-monitoring logs, normally for up to 30 days, with policy, safety or legal exceptions. A zero-retention exemption has not been verified for this project. Deleting content in Vyoora cannot recall a request already sent to a provider or delete a provider’s independent logs.

Save to Photos and Share create a watermarked export without changing the private saved original. iOS requests add-only Photos access. Photos, iCloud, device backups and destinations you choose when sharing are outside Vyoora’s deletion controls. Their own terms and privacy policies apply.

Tracking and the public help website

The described app flows do not use advertising identifiers or cross-app advertising tracking. This privacy and support website has no account, photo upload, analytics script or support form. The hosting service processes requests needed to serve these pages. Contacting support by email shares your message with the support inbox and its email provider so we can respond; please do not send passwords, tokens or private photos.
